# Privacy Policy / 隐私声明

**Scope.** This policy covers the personal, non-commercial WHOOP Developer App "dz-st" and the heartlink / Tavern Bio-Context software it feeds.

- Data accessed: WHOOP recovery, cycle, sleep, workout, profile and body-measurement records of the app owner's own account, and real-time heart rate broadcast over Bluetooth from the owner's own device.
- Storage: all data stays on the owner's own computer (OAuth tokens in a local file with owner-only permissions; readings in the owner's local chat files). No server operated by the author receives any data.
- Sharing: readings are included in prompts sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured, and nowhere else. No analytics, no telemetry, no third parties.
- Retention: the owner can delete local files at any time; revoking the app in WHOOP settings ends access.
- Contact: kcgoofee@outlook.com

**范围。** 本声明适用于个人非商业的 WHOOP 开发者应用 "dz-st" 及其供数的 heartlink / Tavern Bio-Context 软件。所有数据只在使用者本机；不经作者服务器；只随提示词发往使用者自己配置的模型服务商；无统计、无遥测；在 WHOOP 里撤销授权即终止访问。
